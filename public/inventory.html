Spec Key

INTENSITY:Fancy Vivid Yellow (FVY) represents the highest saturation grade for natural fancy color diamonds. Fancy Intense Yellow (FIY) indicates strong color presence with excellent value. Both grades are investment-caliber.

MODIFIERS:Secondary hue descriptors (e.g., Orangy) appear when a stone displays a visible tint beyond pure yellow. These modifiers are disclosed on GIA certificates and affect pricing.

DOCUMENTATION:Upon request, qualified counterparties receive full GIA certification details, provenance documentation, and high-resolution imagery. All stones are conflict-free and sourced through established Mano River channels.

Selection Criteria

Color Character

Every natural fancy color diamond in our inventory undergoes rigorous color grading verification. We focus on Fancy Vivid Yellow and Fancy Intense Yellow stones with consistent saturation and even distribution. Our selection process eliminates stones with unpredictable color zoning or unstable hue modifiers, ensuring your clients receive diamonds with reliable color performance under varied lighting conditions. Each certificate includes precise color origin documentation and secondary hue notation.

Make & Light Performance

Cut quality directly impacts value retention in the fancy color market. We select stones with optimal proportions that maximize color return while maintaining structural integrity. Our make standards emphasize symmetry, polish excellence, and calculated pavilion angles that enhance chromatic brilliance without sacrificing carat weight unnecessarily. Every diamond is evaluated for light leakage, extinction patterns, and scintillation behavior specific to fancy color performance metrics.

Liquidity Considerations

Each acquisition is vetted for certification provenance (GIA, IGI, HRD), origin transparency, and absence of treatment disclosures.
